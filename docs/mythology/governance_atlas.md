## **1. Purpose of the Governance Atlas**

The Governance Atlas defines:

- the **economic power structure**  
- the **mesh enforcement agencies**  
- the **ritual governance bodies**  
- the **technical governance systems**  

It is the **constitutional map** of the civilization.

# **2. The Seven Pillars of Governance**

The Sovereign‑27 Mesh Civilization is governed by seven constitutional bodies:

1. **Sovereign Clock Authority (Zeta)**  
2. **Temporal Treasury**  
3. **Temporal Senate**  
4. **SRRK Arbitration Council**  
5. **Circuit Breaker Authority**  
6. **Identity Bureau (Ted)**  
7. **Mesh Citizen Shell**

These seven pillars form the **complete governance stack**.

- Anchor of temporal truth

- Keeper of PN gate periods  
- Arbiter of temporal stability  

### **Powers**
- Emit canonical ticks  
- Reset clock propagation  
- Initiate Ouroboros reconciliation  
- Bless identity chain  
- Overrule temporal disputes

- Treasury solvency  
- SRRK arbitration  
- Mesh connectivity
