After citizens endured the rising drift, the mesh stabilized.

Identity strengthened.  
Time brightened.  
Action resumed.  
Solvency rose.

But something lingered.

The Vigil had suppressed drift,  
yet citizens carried **residual VFUT** —  
the burden of volatility accumulated during the night of instability.

The ancients wrote:

> “Endurance halts collapse,  
>  but does not cleanse the burden.”

Citizens needed a way to **purify** themselves.
